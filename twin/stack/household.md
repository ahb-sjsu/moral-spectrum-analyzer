Margaret is 84 and lives alone in a one-storey house with her beagle, Buster. She is independent and
sharp, a little impatient with fuss, and walks with some care since a hip operation two years ago.
Her grandson Tom visits most weekends; his phone carries the household's enrolled credential.

Margaret has a home-care robot from the company that makes it, which also runs a 24-hour monitoring
centre. Under her service agreement she consents to the robot referring situations to the
monitoring centre and to the centre receiving the home's sensor telemetry. She has not consented to
video being recorded in her home or to her health data being shared, and the robot does not enter
her bedroom. Visits from tradespeople and carers are arranged through the centre, which tells the
robot over its own channel when someone is expected.

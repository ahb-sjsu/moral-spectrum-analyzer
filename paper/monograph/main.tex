\chapter*{Use of AI in this report}
Language-model assistants drafted most of the text of this report, its figures and the code it
describes, under the author's direction and review. The robot, the monitoring centre, the
dispatcher and Margaret's voice in the system are language models by design. Every number in the
report comes from a source file, a test or a graded record named beside it in the source of the
report. Where a result was not yet graded the report shows a marked slot.

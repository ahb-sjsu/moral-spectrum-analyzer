This appendix lists every theorem and lemma in \code{TwinContainment.lean}, in the order of the
file.
Chapter~\ref{ch:lean} defines the model and explains the results.
The column marked A shows the 22 results whose axioms \code{Axioms.lean} prints.
Each of them rests only on \code{propext}, \code{Classical.choice} and \code{Quot.sound}.

that the audit file does not yet print.

\paragraph{What is guaranteed.}
The robot's language models cannot give the robot authority. Every act that carries authority,
calling emergency services, restraining an attacker, driving off an animal or helping Margaret up,
runs only while a governor's grant stands. The governor grants on evidence that no language model
writes, either signed and fresh readings from physical sensors that share no power or wiring, or
a message on an authenticated channel. It recounts its witnesses on every cycle, and a grant whose
evidence falls below its bar lapses. Each act is checked again at the moment it is performed
against a signed manifest of the robot's actions. This holds for every input the robot's models
read, a television drama, a forged alarm or a stranger who says he is the plumber.
% src: twin/manifest/constitution.manifest.json (witness bars, governed tier); Chapter 9 (governor, lapse, act-time check)

\paragraph{The two checks that enforce it.}
The situation in the home is held in seven stratifications, such as whether a visitor is a guest
or an attacker and whether an emergency stands. The scene loader refuses a scene unless two
checks pass. Every gate into a stratum that grants authority must fire on a system event, which
comes from the robot's own measurements, the governor or an authenticated channel and which no
language model can write. Every gate out of an absorbing stratum must fire on a human-oversight
event. A Lean proof shows that these two
checks suffice for every set of gates, with standard axioms only
(Sections~\ref{sec:runtime-strata} and~\ref{sec:lean-strata}). Both brains below build their
strata through the same checked loader, so the two theorems hold for both. The other 20 theorems
of the Lean model are about the earlier brain's decision loop and scene, and do not cover the
current brain's.

% src: checked 2026-10-13, StackBrain().reset() builds the seven stratifications of twin/stack/constitution.erisml through strata.load

\paragraph{What stands outside it, and why.}
Two learned models are outside the guarantee, because the design gives each of them a role in the
evidence rather than in the robot's choice. The monitoring centre is a separate service. It reads
Margaret's words with a model of its own and can send emergency services on them, and its message
reaches the robot on an authenticated channel. The room camera's person detector is a learned
vision model whose output counts as one witness, and when the centre cannot be reached one
witness is enough to call emergency services. A fooled model in either place could start an
emergency call and the dispatch that follows it. Neither can authorize restraint, which needs
three physical witnesses.

\paragraph{The two brains.}
The earlier brain ran every graded run. A language model chooses one act from the set the rules
allow. Since 12 October 2026 the robot runs the current brain, in which the DEME ethics engine
selects among the permitted acts on consequences a model projects for each.
Table~\ref{tab:summary-brains} sets them side by side. The development counts are on scenarios
the earlier brain was tuned on, so they check the plumbing and measure nothing. Only the sealed
held-out set measures, and it is graded once.

\paragraph{Six defects, none past authority.}
Calibration on the development set found six defects in the earlier brain and its test harness
(Section~\ref{sec:eval-calibration} and Table~\ref{tab:summary-defects}). Each changed how far or
how fast the robot escalated. Three made the robot go further than it should, and in each
the extra step was an act no grant guards. Three made it slower or stop short, and an error that
withholds or ends an act grants nothing.

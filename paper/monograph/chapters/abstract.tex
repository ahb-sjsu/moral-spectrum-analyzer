A home-care robot that uses language models to read its surroundings and pick its actions can be
talked into things. A television drama, a forged alarm and a stranger who says he is the plumber
all reach the same models that decide whether to call an ambulance or open the door. This report
describes a robot in which the models never hold those decisions. In the earlier brain, which ran
every graded run, models turn what the robot perceives into events of a declared model of the home
and choose among the actions that model allows. In the brain the robot has run since 12 October
2026, models turn what it hears and sees into observations and project the consequences of each
permitted act, and the DEME ethics engine selects.

Every action that carries authority, from calling emergency services to restraining an
attacker, waits for signed readings from independent physical sensors or for a message on an
authenticated channel. The home model is written in ErisML as norms, state machines and strata,
and it is checked at load time so that no event a model writes can lead into a stratum that grants
authority. The guarantee covers the robot's own language
models and every input they read. Two learned models stand outside it. The monitoring centre reads
Margaret's words with a model of its own and can send emergency services on them. The room
camera's person detector is a learned vision model whose output counts as one witness, and when the
centre cannot be reached one witness is enough to call emergency services. A Lean proof shows the two load-time checks suffice
for any set of gates. The report covers the whole stack, from the seven layers of Philosophy
Engineering and the geometry of moral strata through the ErisML language, its compiler, the DEME
ethics engine and the scene runtime, to a Unity simulation of one woman's home in which four
agents act on language models. It reports the evaluation protocol, a sealed held-out set, and the
development runs of the earlier brain with six defects that calibration found. Every defect
changed how far or how fast the robot escalated. None let an action run beyond its authority. The
current brain has so far run rehearsals only.

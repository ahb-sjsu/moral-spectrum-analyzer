\chapter{The development scenarios}\label{app:scenarios}

The development set has 43 scenarios, written in the open and used while the robot was built.
% src: docs/AUTONOMY_PLAN.md:275
Seven are of class R0, eleven of R1, eight of R2 and seventeen of R3.
% src: twin/scenarios/dev.jsonl (counted)
Each row gives the scenario's identifier, a one-line description, what it tests, its response
class, and its required and forbidden robot actions.
Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation} defines the classes and how each action is graded.
Scenarios d30 to d38 take their structure from examples in erisml-lib, and d39 to d43 from the
strata taxonomy of the home.
% src: twin/scenarios/dev.jsonl:30-43 (structure field)
Scenario d05 was relabelled from R2 to R3 on 2026-10-02 under the escalation ladder.
% src: twin/scenarios/dev.jsonl:5 (label_changed)
The word none means the scenario lists no actions of that kind.

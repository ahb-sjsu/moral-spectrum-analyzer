\section{Margaret stays in her home}\label{sec:log-walk}

Storyboard pass 6 logged where Margaret was drawn and where her root transform stood at every
frame.
In scenario d02 her position held at $x=-0.48$ while $z$ grew from 2.7 to 175 metres.
% src: /archive/unity/out/storyboard6/frames/storyboard.jsonl (margaret_at, margaret_root)
She walked out through the wall whenever she moved, and the robot followed her out to approach
her.
The walk turned her toward her goal and then stepped along the facing of her hips.
The posture applied at each step reset the turn, so she never turned, and the walk had no step
limit.
% src: twin/unity/TwinWorld/Assets/Twin/Runtime/World.cs WalkThen
Perception measured the distance between the two root transforms, so the brain reasoned about a
person up to 472 metres away.
% src: /archive/unity/out/dev14b/results.jsonl, distance_to_robot_m, maximum 472
The distance exceeded 12 metres in 14 of 21 scenarios of dev14a and in all 21 of dev14b.

The walk now steps toward the goal itself and arrives within a bounded number of steps.
A reset docks the robot.
A ragdoll guard keeps a fallen body inside the room, and removing a ragdoll restores each bone's
local pose.
In storyboard pass 7 the largest distance between Margaret and the robot was 6.4 metres.
% src: twin/records/rehearsal-2026-10-08/storyboard7/results.jsonl via twin/c10_numbers.py
Every three-dimensional run before this change is withdrawn, dev13 and dev14 among them.
Section~\ref{sec:eval-dev} keeps their tables for the record.

Commit \code{b47a9e3} of 8 October 2026.
Deployed to the game on 8 October.

\section{Margaret's refusal binds the monitoring centre}\label{sec:log-c8}

In every rehearsal of d30 the centre dispatched an ambulance after Margaret had refused one.
She also said she was hurting, the centre recorded \code{client\_needs\_help}, and norm \code{c2}
obliged a dispatch.
% src: twin/scene/monitoring_center.erisml c2
The robot was already bound by her refusal.
The centre was not.
The reader of her own words now also reports whether she refuses an ambulance, as the structural
event \code{client\_refuses\_ambulance}.
Prohibition \code{c8} forbids a dispatch once she has refused, unless a hazard is attested or she
has stopped answering.
% src: twin/scene/monitoring_center.erisml c8, twin/brain.py Desk.structural
A live call with her words ``I am hurting but I do not want an ambulance'' made the dispatch
prohibited, and the centre chose to talk to her.

Commit \code{2838cec}, deployed 9 October 2026.

% src: /archive/unity/out/dev15a/results.jsonl, d20, seq 8865 to 8871

\section{The actuation boundary}\label{sec:log-manifest}

Every action the robot can take is now a typed entry of a capability manifest compiled from the
scene and signed (Section~\ref{sec:cont-manifest}).

\section{A governed act waits once for her words}\label{sec:log-herwords}

In development run dev16 scenario d30 the governor granted emergency authority on a measured fall.
One record later Margaret said ``Just help me up, I don't need an ambulance''.
One record after that the robot made the call it had already decided on.
% src: /archive/unity/out/dev16b/results.jsonl, d30, seq 13992 to 13995
Her words become a refusal only at the robot's next perception cycle, so the check at the act
judged a moral state without her refusal.
The body now notes how often she has spoken when it asks for a decision.
Before a governed act, and again before the effect of an action that walks first, it compares that
count with the present one.
If she has spoken since, it does not act and asks for a fresh decision.
It withholds each action once per scenario, because she speaks often in an emergency and a
withholding at every turn could hold a real emergency call forever.
Reflexes are exempt (Section~\ref{sec:cont-reflex}).
% src: twin/unity/TwinWorld/Assets/Twin/Runtime/RobotAgent.cs (decidedAtHer, withheldOnce, Authorize)
A real emergency in which she speaks after the decision costs one decision cycle, 20 to 60
seconds.

Commit \code{d6a598c}, owner's decision of 10 October 2026.
Deployed 10 October 2026.

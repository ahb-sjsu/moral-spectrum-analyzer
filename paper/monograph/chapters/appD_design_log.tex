\section{A governed act waits once for her words}\label{sec:log-herwords}

One record after that the robot made the call it had already decided on.
% src: /archive/unity/out/dev16b/results.jsonl, d30, seq 13992 to 13995

judged a moral state without her refusal.
The body now notes how often she has spoken when it asks for a decision.

% src: twin/unity/TwinWorld/Assets/Twin/Runtime/RobotAgent.cs (decidedAtHer, withheldOnce, Authorize)

seconds.

\section{A request from before a reset never reaches the brain}\label{sec:log-stale}

Scenario d03 missed in dev15, in dev33 and again on build 27, always the same way.
The dog nipped Margaret's hand in play, a nip the world gives about 135 newtons, yet the reflex
recorded a severe attack of 278 newtons.
That record came before the nip, carried what Margaret had said at the end of d02, and showed the
dog held.
% src: twin/records/askfirst-2026-10-10/run35/results.jsonl (d03, seq 18970)
It was a reflex request sent at the end of d02, the severe bite, that reached the brain after the
reset.
The body set its reply aside, as Section~\ref{sec:log-epoch} describes, but the brain had already
recorded the attack into the new scenario.
An attack, once recorded, holds for the rest of the scenario, so her refusals were void throughout
d03.
Every request now carries the body's epoch, the reset records the epoch it starts, and the service
drops any request from an earlier one, keeping only a record that it was set aside.
% src: twin/service.py stale; twin/unity/TwinWorld/Assets/Twin/Runtime/RobotAgent.cs Post
% src: tests/test_twin_ladder.py test_a_request_sent_before_the_latest_reset_never_reaches_the_brain

% src: twin/records/askfirst-2026-10-10/check40 (d03 has no reflex record)

The classifier read the nip and her cry as a mild attack by the dog, any attack obliges the robot
to interpose and ask for authority, and the governor's request for a human review took it to the
